# Workshop slides

This repository is the reveal.js deck for the PEPR Carbon statistical workshop. The deck lives at the repository root (`index.qmd`, `styles.scss`, `sections/`). Do not move it into a `slides/` subdirectory.

## Language and notation

- British English in slide text, speaker notes, captions, and this file.
- One claim per slide. At most two figures. If a slide needs a scrollbar, split it.
- Keep one notation throughout:
  - \(Y\): response
  - \(x\) or \(\mathbf{x}\): covariate or covariate row
  - \(\mu = \mathbb{E}(Y \mid \mathbf{x})\): conditional mean
  - \(g(\mu)\): link
  - \(i\): observational index
  - \(j\): cluster or experimental unit when the treatment sits on the cluster
- When \(Y\) is binary, \(\mu = \pi\). Do not introduce a second letter for the response.

## Figures

- Prefer an existing figure with a known source, or a figure generated by `Rscript scripts/generate-slide-figures.R`.
- Simulated figures are illustrative. Say so on the slide, keep seed `20261005`, and do not describe them as NEON or SPRUCE results.
- Regenerated numbers that appear in the text must match `figures/generated/estimates.json`.
- Crop the twelve-panel poster with `python3 scripts/crop-pitfall-panels.py` rather than re-exporting panels by hand.
- Store borrowed images under `figures/sources/` and generated images under `figures/generated/`. Use `snake_case` file names.
- Do not upscale a source that is too small to read. Redraw it, and point to the original in `figures/sources/` if you still need it.
- Alt text on every image. Credit a figure when the source image carries a credit.

## Citations

- Literature citations come from `Statistical classes.bib`. Do not invent BibTeX entries, citation keys, or DOIs.
- For a source that is not in that file, link the original page or DOI at the point of use.
- Software that only draws a figure does not need a bibliography entry. Name the script instead.

## Build

```bash
Rscript scripts/generate-slide-figures.R
quarto render index.qmd
```

Rendered HTML goes to `docs/` and is published by `.github/workflows/quarto-pages.yml`. Do not commit `docs/`.

Speaker view is `S`. The timing targets are on the section slides: 30, 50, 70, 20, 20, and 30 minutes.
